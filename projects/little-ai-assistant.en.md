# Little AI Assistant: An Independently Developed LLM App

At the end of 2023, I left corporate life and embarked on a journey as a full-time independent developer. Little AI Assistant is the centerpiece of that journey — an all-in-one AI assistant for individual users.

## Why "Little AI Assistant"

After the explosion of LLM capabilities, what was truly missing wasn't models, but products that "turn model capabilities into everyday usable tools". I wanted to build an AI assistant that ordinary users — who don't know Prompt engineering or understand RAG — could pick up and use right away:

- **Ready to use out of the box**: Built-in scenario-based Prompt templates covering high-frequency scenarios like writing, translation, summarization, and learning
- **Knowledge base Q&A**: Based on a RAG architecture, users can feed their own documents and notes to the assistant and get personalized, citation-backed answers
- **Multi-model routing**: Automatically selects the right LLM based on task type and cost, balancing quality and expense

## Technical Architecture

- **Application layer**: Conversational interaction + scenario-based skill plugins
- **Capability layer**: Prompt engineering, RAG (Embedding + vector retrieval + reranking), Function Calling tool use
- **Model layer**: Integrated with multiple mainstream LLM APIs, dynamically routed by task

## The Business Loop

Beyond the product itself, I also closed a complete "AI tool + paid community" loop:

1. Free/low-cost tool to acquire seed users
2. Paid community offering in-depth tutorials and scenario co-creation
3. Community feedback feeding back into product iteration

This experience took me through the entire chain — product, technology, operations, and monetization — and accumulated a wealth of hands-on Prompt/RAG expertise, laying the foundation for my return to enterprise AI products.

# Skylark Smart Pen: write on paper, see it on screen

Skylark is the pen on the Red Dot 2022 page: [YQ·SKYLARK](https://www.red-dot.org/project/yqskylark-59680), made by Beijing 17 Education & Technology Development Co., Ltd. I was responsible for it at that K12 company. An infrared camera reads handwriting on dot-matrix paper and syncs it to the screen. Taking the cap off trips a Hall switch, and a ring light shows the pen is on. The award claim stops at that page. iF Gold and "about 3× content efficiency" still have no public report, so they are not listed as verified results. Scale below uses the figures in the news, not a separate pen-shipment number.

## What the Product Is

Students write on paper with the Skylark Smart Pen, and their handwriting syncs to the cloud in real time, allowing teachers to watch each student's answering process as if it were a live stream. It addresses a core pain point of online education: **teachers can't see the student's thought process — only the final answer**.

- **Paper-and-pen interaction, zero learning curve**: Students answer as usual on paper, no change to writing habits
- **Real-time handwriting sync**: Dot-matrix positioning technology, low-latency pen-track sync to the teacher's screen
- **Process data capture**: Records not just answers, but answering trajectories, pauses, erasures, and other process data

## My Role: Product Owner from 0 to 1

- **Product definition**: Completed the full definition process from market research, user interviews, and competitive analysis to PRD
- **Hardware collaboration**: Deep collaboration with industrial design, structural, and hardware teams, balancing feel, cost, and reliability
- **AI capability delivery**: Drove the integration of handwriting recognition (OCR/HTR) and automatic error-question collection into the product
- **Ecosystem building**: Built the accompanying AI operation platform supporting content production, data dashboards, and teaching operations

## Results

- The Red Dot 2022 page says an infrared camera reads notes on dot-matrix paper and syncs them to the screen. Removing the cap trips a Hall switch. A ring light shows the pen is on. The jury cited natural writing plus easier digitizing.
- The in-house design credit on that page is Junyang Zhang, Qu Chen, and Fan Lin. That is a design credit, not a product-owner list, so the role described here is not rewritten as an award credit.

This experience took me through the full complex product chain of "hardware + AI + education scenarios", and taught me a lasting lesson: a great AI product isn't about showing off technology, but blending seamlessly into the user's existing workflow.

## How schools used it

These reports describe 17 Education's smart paper-and-pen system, the same class of product as YQ·SKYLARK. They do not name "Skylark" or YQ·SKYLARK in every article.

- [People's Daily overseas edition, 13 Oct 2025](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbhwb/pc/content/202510/13/content_30108913.html): in an English listening class at the High School Affiliated to Beijing Normal University, more than 30 students answered with 17Zuoye dot-matrix pens and the writing appeared on the screen.
- [Duozhi, via Sohu, 11 Mar 2022](https://www.sohu.com/a/528965163_112831): Homework 3.0 still uses paper. The notebook carries a dot code and the pen has a camera, so primary students finish at school without a tablet. The article places the system in many Minhang schools and some Xicheng schools. The platform figure in that piece, as of the end of 2020, is about 70,000 schools, more than 1 million teachers, and nearly 60 million students. That is the 17Zuoye platform, not pen shipments.
- [The Paper, 23 Mar 2023](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_22464303): 17 Education won Minhang's smart paper-pen digitization project, worth more than RMB 100 million, aimed at every public primary and secondary school within three years.
- [The Paper, later](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_24797556): the smart pen and Minzhi Homework had reached every Minhang primary and secondary school.
- [Pedaily on the Q2 2024 earnings](https://news.pedaily.cn/202409/539598.shtml): from September 2023 to June 2024, Minhang teachers assigned 224,000 homework tasks, ran 460,000 paper-pen class periods, and held 18,000 assessment sessions on this system.
